Controls

3D view (this page)

  • One finger drag — rotate around the model
  • Two finger drag — slide / pan
  • Pinch or scroll wheel — zoom
  • Desktop: left-drag rotates, right-drag pans

AR mode (after tapping AR)

  • One finger drag — slide model across the surface
  • Two fingers twist — rotate model
  • Pinch — resize model
  • Lift your finger — model anchors; walking then moves you around it
  • Model follows you while walking? You're still dragging — let go to anchor it.
